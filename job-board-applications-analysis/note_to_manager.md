**Cleaning:** removed 6 exact duplicate rows (1,206 → 1,200), standardised 9 work_mode labels (On-site, Remote…), set 15 blank sources to "unknown".

**3. Paid vs unpaid replies.** Median 3 vs 7 days; no reply 13% vs 30%. The gap appears in every role and work mode. Big enough to act on.
